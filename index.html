N

The most influential people in my life, in every aspect — material and moral. The one who's always really there, no matter how much you think you fall short. The only person I can talk to about every part of my life without ever being judged. The best, smartest person who can untangle my scattered thoughts with one small conversation, and somehow — everything gets fixed. Is that magic, or what?

The most understanding person I've ever known, not exaggerating. A passionate person, no matter what life throws at you — and one day everything comes back better than before, and we start chasing our dreams again. A truly responsible person, a mountain walking on earth, who has endured a lot, patiently and quietly. I don't know how, but you carry it all with a light heart.

Let's take a simple walk through the past five years. 21 July 2021, the first time I saw you and wondered, "who is this Nour?" Days passed, and by 29 July we'd done our first thing together, our first real conversation. That's when I realized — unfortunately for me — you were a genuinely good person. 21 September, our first talk on Messenger. Then a first voice note, a first photo of you buried in study mode, a first game, a first WhatsApp chat — and then, unfortunately, time passed and I disappeared.

We found our way back on 6 May 2023, and the conversations picked up again, more and more, until we finally met in person through your friends on 8 July — you had a terrible cold that day, and it bothered me more than it should have. Then came 11 August, a day you planned for me from the second I opened my eyes to the second I closed them. One of the most beautiful, calm days of my life. After that, a whole stretch of staying up late, playing games, being your favorite pest — and one day in particular, 15 January 2024, that I still think about.

By coincidence, 23 December 2024 was your last message before I left for the army. I came back, and by June 2025 we were talking again. Then, completely by chance, I saw you at Roxy on 28 June. And then — 25 July 2025, the day I will never forget. We met again the next day so I could finally get my perfume (yes, mine). A hard stretch followed, one I hope never repeats, for either of us. 20 September was the last time I saw you in person; 22 December, your last message. Then on 13 April 2026, I called you by accident, and we talked again — and here we still are, living inside all of it.

The days pass and everything moves on, but the memories and the words stay exactly where they are, Nour. Truly, in every sense.

Every single year, you're my friend and my companion — the most sincere, most honorable, most wonderful, most stylish, wisest person I know. Happy birthday, Nour. You are, every year, someone it's genuinely never tiring to be around. However much I say, it still won't cover how unique you are. I hope, from my heart, that God eases your heart, brings you joy, and keeps you safe in every step and heard in every prayer.

I'm proud to be part of this journey with you, however far it's gone. However many days, weeks, months, years pass — I hope you're still part of whatever comes next, in some way. You were, you are, and I hope you'll always be one of the best people I've ever known. Not an exaggeration. Truly one of the best things that's happened to me, in every sense.

Happy birthday to the dearest of all.
Every year, the kindest, most beautiful Nour. ♥
